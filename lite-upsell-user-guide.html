Android 年付方案 → 年付升級 Annual → Annual Upgrade

Google Play 不重新起算訂閱週期。升級時只收取「剩餘天數的差額」，原本的到期日完全保留。 Google Play does not restart your billing cycle. You're only charged the prorated difference for remaining days, and your original renewal date is fully preserved.

重點：Google Play 在升級時會建立一筆「過渡訂單」，只收你剩餘天數的費率差額——這不是重新訂閱，你的原始到期日完全不動。 Key insight: When you upgrade, Google Play creates an intermediate "proration order" — charging only the daily rate difference for your remaining days. This is not a new subscription restart; your original renewal date is completely unchanged.
  • ⚡ 立即生效：升級完成後即可使用新方案所有功能。 Instant access: New plan features available immediately after upgrade.
  • 💳 今日扣款：過渡訂單費用（剩餘天數 × 新舊方案每日費率差額）。Google Play 彈窗會顯示精確金額。 Charged today: Proration order (remaining days × daily rate difference). Google Play shows the exact amount in the confirmation dialog.
  • 📅 下次扣款日：原訂閱到期日（原起始日 + 1 年，到期日完全不變）。 Next charge date: Original renewal date (start date + 1 year — completely unchanged).
  • 🔄 新訂閱週期：延續原有年付週期，到期日不變，之後每年自動續訂。 New billing cycle: Continues the existing annual cycle — renewal date unchanged, auto-renews yearly.
試算範例 Example Calculation
假設舊方案每日費率為 $0.066，還剩 200 天，升級至每日費率 $0.099 的新年付方案：
· 舊方案每日費率：$0.066
· 新方案每日費率：$0.099
· 今日差額 = ($0.099 − $0.066) × 200 ≈ $6.60
· 原到期日不變，之後每年按新方案年費續訂。 Say your current plan has a daily rate of $0.066 and you have 200 days left, upgrading to a new annual plan at $0.099/day:
· Current plan daily rate: $0.066
· New plan daily rate: $0.099
· Today's charge = ($0.099 − $0.066) × 200 ≈ $6.60
· Original renewal date unchanged. Future renewals at new plan's annual price.
✓ Google Play 彈窗顯示的就是實際扣款金額，計算是自動的，沒有任何隱藏費用。 The amount shown in the Google Play confirmation dialog is exactly what you'll be charged — the calculation is automatic, with no hidden fees.
